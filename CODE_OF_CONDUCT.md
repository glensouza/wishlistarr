# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Wishlistarr to be a welcoming, respectful project for everyone who takes part, whatever their age, background, experience, identity or ability. Because this project exists to keep children safe, we hold ourselves to an especially high standard of care.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discrimination.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, including real media-server data or screenshots of a family's library, without permission.
- Anything that sexualizes or endangers minors.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately through the repository's Security tab by choosing "Report a vulnerability", or by contacting the maintainer [@glensouza](https://github.com/glensouza) on GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior breaks this code. They will explain the decision to the people involved where it is safe to do so.
